# Flamenco Guitar

## Rhythm, Passion, and Precision

Flamenco guitar is a living art form that blends **power**, *emotion*, and **technical control**. Every phrase carries a story, every strum reflects a pulse, and every performance feels alive.

### What makes it special?

- **Compás** gives each piece its heartbeat
- *Rasgueado* creates fiery texture and attack
- **Tremolo** shapes the melody with shimmer
- *Golpe* adds sharp, percussive accents
- **Palmas** and footwork bring the audience into the rhythm

---

## Essential Flamenco Techniques

### Unordered list

- Rasgueado: fast, dramatic strumming
- Tremolo: sustained, singing melody
- Arpeggio: beautiful fingerstyle patterns
- Golpe: tapping on the guitar body
- Picado: fast melodic runs

### Ordered list

1. Learn the compás before the melody
2. Practice rasgueado with relaxed hand movement
3. Build tremolo control for sustained phrases
4. Add golpe and palmas for rhythm and feel
5. Play with emotion, not just speed

## Common Flamenco Forms

| Form | Feel | Character |
|------|------|-----------|
| Soleares | Deep, soulful | Meditative and expressive |
| Bulerías | Fast and fiery | Energetic and playful |
| Tangos | Lively and elegant | Rhythmic and smooth |
| Fandangos | Warm and lyrical | Gentle and expressive |
| Alegrías | Bright and festive | Joyful and upbeat |

---

## Where the Music Comes From

Flamenco began in Andalusia, influenced by **Spanish**, *Romani*, *Arabian*, and *African* musical traditions. Its power comes from the balance between rhythm, improvisation, and emotion.
